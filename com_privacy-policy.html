Google may use this information for advertising, ad measurement and SDK analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

The Applications do not currently use a separate analytics service. Any analytics described above are part of the Google Mobile Ads SDK and its operation.
