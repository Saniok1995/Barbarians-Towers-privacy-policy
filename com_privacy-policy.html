Information We Do Not Ask You to Provide

The Applications do not require account registration and do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, payment information, or other contact details directly to the Service Provider. The Service Provider does not maintain server-based user accounts. Local app data and information processed for purchases and subscriptions are described below.

Information Collected Through Advertising

The Applications use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to display advertisements. When the SDK is permitted to request ads, it may automatically collect and share the following information with Google:

  • your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address, which may be used to estimate the device's general location;
  • product interaction information, such as app launches, taps, and advertisement or video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage; and
  • device and account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID, App Set ID, and, where applicable, other identifiers associated with accounts signed in on the device.

Google may use this information for advertising, ad measurement and SDK analytics, security, and fraud prevention. Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS).

The Applications do not currently use a separate analytics service. Any analytics described above are part of the Google Mobile Ads SDK and its operation.

Advertising Choices and Consent

Where required by applicable law, the Applications use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your privacy choices before requesting advertisements. Depending on your location and choices, Google may provide personalized, non-personalized, or limited advertisements.

Where privacy options are required, you can review or change your choices at any time through the privacy settings or privacy options available in the relevant Application. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that occurred before the withdrawal. You can also reset or delete your Android advertising ID using your device's privacy settings.

Purchases and Subscriptions

This section applies to Pounce: Cat Toy, Game for Cats (com.BlackSwan.Cattoy), which offers optional paid subscriptions through Google Play Billing using Unity In-App Purchasing (Unity IAP). Google Play processes payments; the Application and the Service Provider do not receive your full payment card details or bank account credentials.

The Application receives purchase information, such as product identifiers, purchase tokens or receipts, and purchase and subscription status, to confirm purchases and provide or restore access to premium features. Google and Unity process information needed to operate their purchasing services. Unity IAP may also process installation and player identifiers, device information, session identifiers, and country information, depending on the SDK version and configuration. This processing is separate from the advertising-related analytics described above.

Pounce stores local app data on your device, including settings, tutorial progress, launch counts, and premium access status. This local data does not require you to register an account with the Service Provider. You can remove it by clearing the Application's storage in your device settings.

Purchase and subscription records held by Google and Unity are subject to their retention policies and applicable legal obligations. Clearing local data or uninstalling the Application does not delete those records or cancel a subscription. You can manage or cancel your subscription through Google Play. For access or deletion requests concerning purchase-related data processed on the Service Provider's behalf, contact aliaksandr.pauliuchyk@gmail.com; the Service Provider will coordinate with the relevant provider as needed.

For more information about these third-party services, see the Google Privacy Policy, Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy, and Unity IAP privacy documentation.
